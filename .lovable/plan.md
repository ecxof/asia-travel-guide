  - best time to go

  - one memorable “interesting fact”

- The homepage should preview everything, while destination pages provide depth.
- Include prominent CTAs like “Take the quiz” and “Explore this destination.”

7. Make mobile the default experience
- Ensure the hero, cards, quiz, and destination sections stack cleanly on narrow screens.
